# Sources & Fact Basis — "The Man on Somerton Beach"

Every factual claim spoken or shown in this documentary is drawn from the sources below.
The film deliberately separates **evidence** (labelled EXHIBIT) from **speculation** (red
SPECULATION stamp), and it never fabricates archival photographs — every "photograph" is a
labelled in-frame reconstruction drawn in code.

## Primary sources used for verification

1. **Wikipedia — "Somerton Man"** (retrieved 11 October 2026) — consolidated, cited account of the
   discovery, autopsy, inquest, suitcase, Tamám Shud paper, the Rubaiyat and its cipher, Jessica
   Thomson and Alf Boxall, the Abbott investigation, the 2021 exhumation and the 2022 identification
   claim. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerton_Man
2. **The Advertiser (Adelaide), 2 December 1948, p. 3** — "Body found on Beach" (as quoted by the
   Wikipedia article: first report, discovery credited to Mr J. Lyons of Whyte Rd, Somerton).
3. **The Independent, 27 July 2022** — "Researchers claim to have solved mystery of 'Somerton man'"
   (Abbott & Fitzpatrick announcement; DNA from hair in the death mask; note on the unverified status).
   https://www.the-independent.com/news/world/australasia/somerton-man-mystery-identity-solved-b2230625.html
4. **Smithsonian Magazine** — "The Enduring Mystery of the Somerton Man, One of Australia's Most
   Puzzling Cold Cases" (context; Cold War theories; Carl Webb claim).
   https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/the-enduring-mystery-somerton-man-one-of-australias-most-puzzling-cold-cases-50795611/
5. **ABC News / Australian Story (Nov 2022)** — publication of the 1920s Webb family photographs and
   the research narrative around the identification (as cited by Wikipedia).
6. **Cipher Foundation — "Tamam Shud / Somerton Man"** — analysis of the code's status (unbroken;
   likely initials; insufficient symbols).
   https://cipherfoundation.org/modern-ciphers/tamam-shud-somerton-man
7. **Gerry Feltus, "The Unknown Man" (2010)** — cold-case detective's account (as cited by Wikipedia
   for the Thomson interview, the book-find timing, and the "evasive" characterization).

## Notes on contested or time-sensitive claims

- The identification of the man as **Carl "Charles" Webb** (26 July 2022, Derek Abbott and Colleen
  Fitzpatrick, via DNA from hair preserved in the 1949 plaster death mask and genetic genealogy) is
  presented in the film **as a claim**: South Australia Police and Forensic Science South Australia
  had not verified it in the available reporting; police described themselves as "cautiously
  optimistic". The film repeats this caveat on screen.
- The **timing of the Rubaiyat's discovery** (a week or two before the body was found, per one
  contemporary newspaper, versus "just after", per Feltus) is presented as the recorded
  contradiction it is.
- The **cause of death** was never determined; the coroner's poison hypothesis (glucoside) and the
  Hicks Exhibit C.18 drugs (later identified as digitalis and ouabain) are presented as inquest
  evidence, not conclusions.
- The cipher's five lines are shown exactly as transcribed by the Wikipedia article
  (WRGOABABD / MLIAOI struck out / WTBIMPANETP / MLIABOAIAQC / ITTMTSAMSTGAB, with an X above the
  final O of the fourth line); the 1978 Defence cryptographers' assessment and the 2014 Rehling
  analysis are quoted in summary.
- Direct quotations on screen: John Dwyer ("I am quite convinced the death could not have been
  natural"), Lionel Leane ("completely taken aback, to the point of giving the appearance that she
  was about to faint"), the West Terrace grave marker ("Here lies the unknown man who was found at
  Somerton Beach"), and the June 1949 journalist's line ("made certain that the glass would be
  empty, save for speculation").
